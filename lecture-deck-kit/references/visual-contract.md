# Lecture Deck Kit: Visual Contract

This kit is anchored to the latest VueConf deck in the talks repository:

- Reference slides: talks/packages/2026-07-18-vueconf-agent-vue-way/slides.md
- Reference style: talks/packages/2026-07-18-vueconf-agent-vue-way/style.css

## Communication and composition

- One slide has one narrative job and one primary claim.
- Prefer a short takeaway title over a topic label.
- Open with a tension, question, or concrete context.
- Close with an actionable synthesis, application, or memorable formula.
- Vary silhouettes: title, divider, media, comparison, process, demo, practice, takeaway.
- Do not repeat a dense card grid as the default layout.
- Keep text short; shorten copy before shrinking type.

## Visual language

- Near-black stage with restrained cyan, blue, green, and amber accents.
- White primary text, muted gray secondary text, and low-contrast borders.
- Large typography with generous horizontal margins.
- Use one composition rather than a dashboard of small panels.
- Use rounded media frames and subtle borders, not heavy decoration.
- Prefer real screenshots, diagrams, videos, and meaningful logos over generic stock decoration.

## Motion

- Use fade-out for slide-to-slide transitions.
- Use opacity, blur, and a small translate for staged reveals.
- Let motion explain sequence or emphasis.
- Avoid bouncing, noisy perpetual motion, and decorative WebGL backgrounds.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Icons and brands

- Use Iconify SVG classes from the installed icon sets.
- Choose one coherent icon family within a slide.
- Use product or company logos only when they carry meaning.
- Prefer logos and symbols over emoji for technical content.

## Output constraints

- Target Slidev web presentation.
- Keep local assets under public/.
- Keep asset provenance in the brief or speaker notes.
- Build and visually inspect before considering a deck complete.
- Do not invent claims, numbers, sources, or company policies.
